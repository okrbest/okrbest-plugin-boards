# Product Definition

## Initial Concept
Mattermost Boards Plugin

## Product Vision
To provide a robust, open-source, and self-hosted project management tool deeply integrated into the Mattermost platform. It aims to offer a seamless alternative to Trello, Notion, and Asana, empowering teams to manage tasks and projects without leaving their communication hub.

## Target Audience
- **Collaborative Teams:** Teams already using Mattermost for communication who need integrated project management.
- **Privacy-Conscious Organizations:** Organizations requiring self-hosted solutions for data sovereignty and security.
- **Developers & DevOps:** Users who prefer open-source tools and extensibility.

## Core Features
- **Kanban Boards:** Visual task management with drag-and-drop capabilities.
- **Rich Text Editing:** Advanced content creation using BlockSuite integration.
- **Mattermost Integration:** Seamless syncing with Mattermost channels and threads.
- **Multi-View Support:** Table, Board, Calendar, and Gallery views for diverse data representation.
- **Multilingual Support:** Localization for global team accessibility.

## Key Goals
- Deliver a stable and performant plugin experience within Mattermost.
- Maintain high compatibility with Mattermost Server versions.
- Enhance the editing experience through BlockSuite migration.
